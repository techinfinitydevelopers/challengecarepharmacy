FAQ

Questions? We’re here to help.

What conditions do you treat?

We provide infusion, injectable and subcutaneous therapies for a wide range of chronic and acute conditions — including autoimmune disorders, neurological conditions, immunodeficiencies and more.

Can I receive treatment at home?

Yes. Depending on your therapy, insurance coverage and clinical needs, treatment can be given in the comfort of your home or at one of our infusion centers.

Will my insurance cover infusion therapy?

We work directly with your insurer to verify benefits, confirm coverage and explain any out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins — and keep you informed at every step.

Do I need a referral from my healthcare provider?

Yes. A referral from your healthcare provider is required. Our team works closely with your provider to coordinate the documentation and approvals.

Can I switch from another infusion provider?

Yes. We can coordinate your transition from a hospital, physician office, home infusion provider or another infusion center.

What happens at my first infusion appointment?

Your care team reviews your treatment, answers your questions and explains what to expect. You’re monitored throughout so you stay comfortable before, during and after.
